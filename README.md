Project Idea:  Store Sales 
This project turns four years of retail order data into business insights, sales forecasts and an interactive reports & dashborads that helps decision makers plan sales, products and shipping.
Project Description:
The dataset covers a US retail store from January 2015 to December 2018.
•	Size: 9,800 order lines, 4,922 orders, 793 customers, 1,861 products, 49 states.
•	Total sales: about $2.26 million.
•	Main fields: order and ship dates, ship mode, customer and segment, location (region, state, city), product (category, sub-category, name) and sales amount.
Project Goal:
Give decision makers a clear view of what drives sales and what to expect next, through clean data, evidence-based analysis, accurate forecasts, reports and a simple dashboard.
Objectives:
1.	Build a clean, well-structured data model ready for analysis.
2.	Find the best and worst performing categories, regions, segments, customers and periods.
3.	Measure the combined impact of product category and region on sales.
4.	Forecast sales for the next 6–12 months.
5.	Deliver dashboard, a final report and a presentation with practical recommendations.
